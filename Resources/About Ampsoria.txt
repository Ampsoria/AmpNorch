AmpNotch 1.2 — Ampsoria

Native macOS notch with Apple Music/Spotify controls, paired browser media controls,
local system-audio waveform with artwork colors, AirPods connection/proximity HUDs,
and StandBy with actual media, battery, time and weather data.

Apple Liquid Glass requires macOS 26. Live audio requires macOS 14.2 and System
Audio Recording permission. Browser integration requires the companion extension.
AirPods case broadcasts vary by hardware/firmware and are not guaranteed for all models.
StandBy is an app window, not a secure lock screen. There is no call answering or
video mirroring. See RELEASE_NOTES.md and INTEGRATIONS.md for verified behavior.
